Imposition

Use this menu to make settings for printing a single document page over multiple sheets.

Available functions differ depending on the printer or printer driver you are using.

Depending on the printer you are using, this function may not be available.

Restore Defaults

Restores the settings on this menu to the default settings.

Poster:

Prints a single document page printed over multiple sheets.

Splitting the Document Image across Multiple Sheets (Poster)

Pamphlet:

Arranges eight pages on one sheet using a layout suitable for pamphlets.
Cut and fold the sheet according to the lines to make a pamphlet.

Printing Eight Pages on a Sheet for Making Pamphlets

Draw Border

Select the check box to have document pages delineated by a dotted line.

Imposition (Speed):

Prints two pages of a document side by side on one sheet of paper. The sheet can then be cut in half and stacked in the specified order (left to right/right to left/top to bottom) to make one copy of a document.

Printing a Document with Imposition Methods

Imposition (Double):

Prints two copies of a document side by side on one sheet of paper. The sheet can then be cut in half to make two copies of a document.

Printing a Document with Imposition Methods
