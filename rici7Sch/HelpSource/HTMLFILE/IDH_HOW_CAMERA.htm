Correcting the Hue of the Digital Photos

This describes how to correct the hue of photos taken with a digital camera. If there are extreme light and shade differences between the object and background, the object may be too dark or too light. This function is effective for those types of photos.

Note:

  • This function is available only with color printers.

  • This function may not be effective for all image data.

Exposure

Exposure refers to the levels of light in which the photo was taken. If there are extreme light and shade differences between the object and background, the object may be under exposed (too dark) or over exposed (too light).

  1. Display photo data taken with a digital camera in an application.

  2. Open the printing preferences dialog box.

  3. Click the Print Quality:Advanced menu on the Detailed Settings tab.

  4. Select the Adjust Photographs check box.

  5. After making any other necessary settings, click OK to close the printing preferences dialog box.

  6. Start printing.
