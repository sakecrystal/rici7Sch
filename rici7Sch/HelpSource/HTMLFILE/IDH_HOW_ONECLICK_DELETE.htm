Deleting a One Click Preset

Follow these steps to delete a one click preset.

Note:

  • You can only delete one click presets with the icon.

  1. Open the printing preferences dialog box.

  2. Click Manage... on the Frequently Used Settings tab. The Manage One Click Preset List dialog box appears.

  1. Select the one click preset name which you want to delete, and then click Delete.

  • You cannot delete the system-provided one click preset.

  1. When a confirmation message appears, click Yes.

  2. Click OK to close the Manage One Click Preset List dialog box.
