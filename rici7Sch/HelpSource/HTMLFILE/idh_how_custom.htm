Printing on Custom Size Paper

Use the following procedure to print with a custom paper size.

Note:

  • A paper size mismatch error may occur due to a miscalculation in the paper size.

Printing on Custom Size Paper Whose Size Has Not Been Registered Yet

  1. Open the printing preferences dialog box after creating a document in an application.

  2. Click the Frequently Used Settings tab or the Basic menu on the Detailed Settings tab.

  3. Click Custom Paper Size... to open the dialog box.

  • Click the << Collapse list button if the Custom Paper Size... button on the Frequently Used Settings tab is not displayed.

  1. Enter the length and width of the paper as seen in the graphic.

  • Enter the size of the paper edge that is vertical relative to the feed direction of the printer in Width:.

  • Enter the size of the paper edge that is horizontal relative to the feed direction of the printer in Length:.

  1. Click OK to close the dialog box.

  2. Place paper on a tray compatible with the custom paper size.

  3. Returning back to your computer, make any other necessary settings, and then click OK.
    The printing preferences dialog box closes.

  4. Start printing.

Printing on Registered Custom Size Paper

Preparation:

  • Register the size of custom size paper that is loaded in the printer properties dialog box in advance. For details, see Registering Custom Paper Sizes.

  1. Open the printing preferences dialog box after creating a document in an application.

  2. Click the Frequently Used Settings tab or the Basic menu of the Detailed Settings tab.

  3. In the Document Size: list, select the size of custom size paper you want to print on. The custom paper sizes available in the list are those that have been registered in advance.

  4. Place paper on a tray compatible with the custom paper size.

  5. Returning back to your computer, make any other necessary settings, and then click OK.
    The printing preferences dialog box closes.

  6. Start printing.
