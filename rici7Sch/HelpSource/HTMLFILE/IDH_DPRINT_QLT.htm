Print Quality:Standard

Use this menu to adjust the standard settings that can affect the output quality of the print jobs.

Available functions differ depending on the printer or printer driver you are using.

Restore Defaults

Restores the settings on this menu to the default settings.

Adjust Image Quality/Color

Correct the image quality and color tone to make the optimum setting for printing.

Standard Settings

Select the type of your document to print it with optimal settings for the type.

The available items vary depending on the printer you are using.

Generic Documents

Prints with settings suitable for a standard document, such as that generated by a wordprocessor or spreadsheet application.

Photographic

Prints with settings suitable for documents containing fine gradations in tone, such as photographs.

Note:

  • Depending on the application you are using, images and graphics might be printed in full color (CMYK), even in black and white documents.

DTP

Prints with settings suited to reproduction of graphics.

Note:

  • Depending on the application you are using, images and graphics might be printed in full color (CMYK), even in black and white documents.

POP Display

Prints the color red more vividly. It is an optimal setting for the POP display.

CAD

Enhances reproduction of fine colored lines, for example, when printing from CAD applications are performed.

Advanced Settings

Allows you to configure the image quality and color tone manually.

Details...

Open the Details dialog box and set the details.

Resolution:

Select a resolution. The higher resolution will result in better print quality but may cause printing to take longer.

Note:

  • Resolutions are listed in reverse order of quality (lowest first).

  • If you print large-sized or high-resolution images, the printing might not be processed due to low memory. In this case, select a lower print resolution than the one you selected.

Toner Saving:

Select a toner saving mode.

The available items vary depending on the printer you are using.

Off

Prints without toner saving mode.

On

Reduces the amount of toner used when printing is performed.

Toner Saving 1

Reduces the amount of toner used when printing is performed.

Toner Saving 2

Further reduces the amount of toner used when printing is performed (more reduced than when Toner Saving 1 is selected). While this increases the life of the toner cartridge, the output appears slightly lighter.

Medium Light

Reduces the amount of toner used when printing is performed.

Light

Further reduces the amount of toner used when printing is performed (more reduced than when Medium Light is selected). While this increases the life of the toner cartridge, the output appears slightly lighter.

User Setting

Allows you to make detailed settings in the Toner Save User Settings dialog box.

Barrier-free Color Management:

Specify whether to convert the colors used in documents to the barrier-free colors applicable to Color Universal Design (CUD).

The color tone of the print result might differ considerably from the color tone of the original.

Print Priority:

Select the method you want to use for rendering bitmaps. When documents containing high resolution bitmap images are printed, printing takes longer due to the large amount of data that must be processed.

Normal

Prints with a balance of quality and speed.

Quality

Prints with quality as priority.

Speed

Printing is fast.

High Speed

Prints with the priority on print speed.

Two-color:

Prints in two colors.

Select the combination of toner colors.

This function is available only if Advanced Settings is selected in Adjust Image Quality/Color.
