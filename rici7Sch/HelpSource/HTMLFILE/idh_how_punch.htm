Punching Prints

When a printer is equipped with a finisher that has the punch function, holes can be punched in the printed documents.

Depending on the printer you are using, this function may not be available.

Note:

  • If the Punch: list is grayed out, check that the optional finisher is installed on the printer and selected on the Accessories tab of the printer properties dialog box.

  • If the punch position you require is not available, check the type of punch unit selected in the Punch Unit: list on the Accessories tab.

  • Available punch positions depend on the settings selected in Orientation and Print On: on the Frequently Used Settings tab or the Basic menu of the Detailed Settings tab. Check these settings in advance.

  1. Open the printing preferences dialog box after creating a document in an application.

  2. Click the Frequently Used Settings tab or the Finishing menu of the Detailed Settings tab.

  3. Select the punching position in the Punch: list.

  4. After making any other necessary settings, click OK to close the printing preferences dialog box.

  5. Start printing.
