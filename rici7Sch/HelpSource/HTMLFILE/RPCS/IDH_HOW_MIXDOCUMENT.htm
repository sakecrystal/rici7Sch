Changing the Page Rotation Direction for Mixed Orientation Documents

Some applications allow the page size and orientation of a single document to be changed. When documents containing a mixture of landscape and portrait pages are printed, document pages whose orientation differs from the paper orientation are printed rotated by 90 degrees. You can change the direction in which the images are rotated.

Note:

  • If you select Printer Defaults, you can specify the print direction settings even when layout-printing or booklet-printing is selected in the 2 Sided/Layout/Booklet menu. When you apply layout-printing or booklet-printing to documents containing a mixture of landscape and portrait pages, document pages whose orientation differs from the paper orientation are rotated or not rotated but reduced in size. Therefore, no blank pages are output.

  1. Open the printing preferences dialog box after creating a document in an application.

  2. Click the Output Settings menu on the Detailed Settings tab.

  3. Click Rotate Mixed Original Orientation... to open the Rotate Mixed Original Orientation dialog box.

  4. Select Printer Defaults, Rotate to a naturally readable direction, or Rotate to reverse readable direction.

  1. Click OK to close the dialog box.

  2. After making any other necessary settings, click OK to close the printing preferences dialog box.

  3. Start printing.
