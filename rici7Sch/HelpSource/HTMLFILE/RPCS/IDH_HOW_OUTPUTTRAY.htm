Selecting the Paper Tray

When Auto Tray Select is selected, a paper tray containing paper of the appropriate size and type will be selected automatically. However, if several paper trays are loaded with paper of the same size, your document might not be printed on the paper you intend. To make sure that the correct paper is selected, you can specify the tray containing paper to print onto before printing.

Available functions differ depending on the printer or printer driver you are using.

  1. Open the printing preferences dialog box after creating a document in an application.

  2. Click the Paper menu on the Detailed Settings tab.

  3. In the Input Tray: list, select the paper tray containing the paper you want to print onto.

  4. If Bypass Tray is selected, click the Position in bypass tray... button to open the printing preferences dialog box.

  5. Select the direction of paper loaded in the bypass tray.

  6. Click OK to close the dialog box.

  7. After making any other necessary settings, click OK to close the printing preferences dialog box.

  8. Start printing.
